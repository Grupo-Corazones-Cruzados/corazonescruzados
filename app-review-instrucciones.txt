whatsapp_business_messaging — «Describe cómo tu app usa este permiso»
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

>>>
Our platform hosts an AI assistant that answers WhatsApp messages on behalf of
our business customers. Each customer connects their own WhatsApp Business
number through Embedded Signup and remains the owner of that number and of the
conversations; we act strictly as their data processor.

We use whatsapp_business_messaging for exactly two things:

1. RECEIVING. We subscribe to the `messages` webhook field for the customer's
   WhatsApp Business Account so that inbound messages from their end customers
   reach our platform. Each message is stored against that customer's channel
   only, and is shown in their inbox.

2. REPLYING. Our assistant composes an answer using the knowledge base that the
   business itself configured, and we send it back to the same end customer
   through the Cloud API (POST /{phone-number-id}/messages), using the access
   token issued for that specific customer. A human operator on the business
   side can take over a conversation at any time and reply manually from the
   same inbox; while a conversation is taken over, the assistant stays silent so
   the end customer never receives two answers.

Free-form replies go only to people who messaged the business first, inside the
customer service window. Outside it we send only Meta-approved message templates,
from the business's own number, to contacts the business already holds and from
whom it has obtained consent. We never message people on behalf of a business
that has not connected its own number through Embedded Signup and granted us
access, and we do not sell or share contact lists.

>>>
WHO USES THIS APP
There are two kinds of people involved. First, our business customers: small and
medium organizations in Ecuador — a tour operator, a clinic, a store — who
already run their day-to-day customer service over WhatsApp. Second, their end
customers: the people who write to that business asking about prices, schedules,
availability or how to get started.

HOW THE APP USES whatsapp_business_messaging
The business connects its own WhatsApp Business number through Embedded Signup.
It remains the owner of the number and of every conversation; we act strictly as
its data processor. From that point the permission is used for exactly two
operations:

1. RECEIVING. We subscribe to the `messages` webhook field for that customer's
   WhatsApp Business Account. When one of their end customers writes, the message
   arrives at our webhook, we verify the payload signature, and we store it
   against that customer's channel only. It then appears in that business's
   inbox inside our platform.

2. REPLYING. An AI assistant composes an answer using only the knowledge base
   that the business itself wrote and configured — its services, its schedule,
   its policies. We send that answer back to the same end customer through the
   Cloud API (POST /{phone-number-id}/messages), using the access token issued
   for that specific business. At any moment a human on the business side can
   press "take over the conversation": the assistant then goes silent for that
   thread and the person replies manually from the same inbox, so the end
   customer never receives two answers to one question.

WHAT VALUE THIS BRINGS
For the end customer, the value is being answered. A small business in Ecuador
cannot staff WhatsApp at 9 p.m. or on a Sunday, and the ordinary outcome of
writing to one is waiting until the next working day — or being forgotten. With
this permission the answer arrives in seconds, at any hour, in Spanish, with the
business's real information rather than a generic auto-reply. And when the
question needs a person, the handover is immediate and happens in the same
conversation: the end customer never has to repeat themselves or move to another
channel.

For the business, the value is keeping the channel their customers already use.
Because the number is connected with coexistence, their team keeps WhatsApp on
their phones and WhatsApp Web exactly as before — nothing is taken away from
them. They gain an assistant that covers the hours they cannot, a shared inbox
where the whole team sees the same history, and the ability to step in whenever
they want.

WHY IT IS NECESSARY FOR THE APP TO WORK
This app is a WhatsApp inbox with an assistant attached; without
whatsapp_business_messaging there is no app. The permission is what allows
messages to reach us at all — without the `messages` webhook subscription, not a
single message is delivered — and what allows any answer to be sent back. There
is no alternative path: the WhatsApp Cloud API is the only supported way to send
and receive on a business's own WhatsApp Business number, and a business cannot
delegate its customer service to software without it.

HOW WE STAY WITHIN THE POLICY
Free-form replies are only ever sent to people who messaged the business first,
and only inside the 24-hour customer service window — this is the assistant's
normal mode of operation.

Outside that window we send only APPROVED MESSAGE TEMPLATES, which is what
templates exist for. A business can select one of its own Meta-approved templates
and send it to a list of its own contacts — for example a confirmation, an
appointment reminder or a service notice. Those templates are reviewed and
approved by Meta before they can be used at all, they are always sent from the
business's own number, and the recipients are contacts that the business already
holds and from whom it has obtained consent. Each message is recorded in the same
inbox, labelled as a template send, so the business has a full record of what was
sent and to whom.

We do not sell or share contact lists, we do not send on behalf of third parties,
and we never message anyone on behalf of a business that has not connected its own
number through Embedded Signup and explicitly granted us access. Each business's
access token is stored encrypted, bound to that channel, and used only for that
business's own number. End customers can ask for their data to be deleted through
the process published at
https://app.grupocc.org/legal/whatsapp#eliminar-datos

whatsapp_business_management — «Describe cómo tu app usa este permiso»
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

>>>
We use whatsapp_business_management only to set up and maintain the connection
between a business customer's WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) and our platform,
never to read or manage anything beyond what that setup requires:

1. ONBOARDING. When a business connects through Embedded Signup, we exchange the
   returned authorization code for that customer's access token.

2. SUBSCRIBING OUR APP TO THEIR WABA (POST /{waba-id}/subscribed_apps) and then
   verifying the subscription actually took effect. Without this step no message
   ever reaches us, and the setup would look successful while silently failing.

3. READING THE STATE OF THEIR NUMBER (GET /{waba-id}/phone_numbers): display
   number, verified name, status and quality rating. This is what we show the
   business on its own connection screen, and what tells us whether the
   onboarding still needs work on their side.

4. REGISTERING A PHONE NUMBER with the Cloud API when required
   (POST /{phone-number-id}/register), so the number can send and receive.

We do not use this permission to create, modify or delete assets in a customer's
portfolio, to manage users or roles, or to access any WhatsApp Business Account
other than one whose owner explicitly connected it to us through Embedded Signup.
Each customer's token is stored encrypted and is used only for that customer's
own account.

public_profile — «Describe cómo tu app usa este permiso»
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

>>>
We do not use profile data for any product feature.

public_profile is included by default with Facebook Login for Business, which we
use solely to run the WhatsApp Embedded Signup flow: a business owner signs in
with their own Facebook account so that Meta can show them their portfolios and
WhatsApp Business Accounts and let them authorize the connection. The only value
we take out of that flow is the authorization code, which we exchange for the
customer's WhatsApp access token.

We do not read, store, display or share any profile field (name, ID, picture),
we do not use it to create or identify accounts in our platform — sign-in to our
platform is our own email and password system — and we do not share it with any
third party. This is why this permission shows no test API calls beyond the
single verification call.
